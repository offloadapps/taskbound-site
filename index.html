I

Offload

Cast it all into the Well.

Type it the way you think, a whole list at once. Taskbound sorts out the dates, repeats, reminders and sizes, and your tasks rise back up as runestones.

"bins out friday 6pm, remind me 30 mins before"

  • No forms, no fiddling with dates
  • Say it out loud on Android
II

Your tasks

Every task is a candle.

A taller candle is a bigger task. Tap it to light it, and it burns on with the rest of what you got done today. Later days wait quietly in the dark.

  • Today first, then what's coming
  • Lists, repeats and gentle reminders
  • Big tasks earn more
III

The Hollow Keep

Embers wake the keep.

Finished tasks give you embers. Offer them to the keep and its rooms wake one window at a time. Watch the keep behind these words: it wakes as you read.

Eight rooms to bring back

Go in. Make it home.

Every woken room can be entered. It starts dusty and cold, and remembers being loved. Each thing you bring for it brings it back a little, from Dusty to Swept to Lived in.

Your hero

Who wakes the keep?

Choose who you are and give them a name. They carry your finished tasks out into the world, and wear everything you earn.

IV

Journeys and the Market

Send them out. Bring back silver.

Finished tasks pack provisions. Spend them to send your hero across the star chart, to the Hush Garden, the Drowned Mill and the Glass Ruins. They come home with silver and sometimes a small found thing.

  • Furnish your rooms at the Market
  • Armour, weapons and trinkets that make journeys shorter and richer
V

Dark skies, kind rules

Nothing here ever punishes you.

  • No streaks, nothing expiresNothing is ever late and nothing is lost. A quiet week just means the keep waits for you.
  • Small steps countTiny tasks earn embers too. Replying to one message is a real thing done.
  • Stays on your phoneNo account. Your tasks are saved on your device and never sent anywhere.
