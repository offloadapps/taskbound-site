Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026. Applies to the Taskbound app for Android and the Taskbound web version.

The short version: Taskbound does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your tasks, your keep and your hero are saved only on your own device.

What Taskbound stores, and where

Everything you add to Taskbound is saved on your device and nowhere else. That includes your tasks, reminders, settings, your hero's name and your progress. We (the developer) never receive a copy and cannot see it.

  • Android app: saved in the app's private storage on your phone. The app does not have permission to use the internet.
  • Web version: saved in your browser's local storage on that device.

Deleting the app or clearing its data removes everything.

Android backup

If you have turned on Google backup on your phone, Android may include Taskbound's save in your phone's backup, so your progress comes back on a new phone. That backup is made and stored by Google under your Google account and Google's privacy policy. We cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

Permissions the Android app uses

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you choose in Settings, and to tell you when your hero is back from a journey. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Run at startup: to set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
  • Alarms and reminders: only if you turn on exact reminders in Settings, so reminders arrive at the exact time you chose. Android asks you first.

Speaking into the Well

When you tap the microphone in the Well, Taskbound asks your phone's own speech recognition service to turn what you say into text. That service is provided by your phone's maker or Google, not by Taskbound, and works under their privacy terms. Taskbound only receives the finished text, which is saved on your device like any other task. Taskbound never records or keeps audio.

Sharing into Taskbound

If you share text to Taskbound from another app, it opens in the Well on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The website

This website and the web version are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical information, such as IP addresses, when pages are loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and its fonts are served from this site.

Children

Taskbound is made for teens and adults. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Taskbound ever starts collecting any data, this policy will say so clearly before that happens.
